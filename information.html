💪 Welcome to my Information page! 💪

Dandys World is a multiplayer survival game created by BlushCrush Studio. Players complete floors by completing machines to assist themselves into higher floors.

Toons have six quests called mastery which when completed, they recieve a skin which is grey, black and white which is called a "Vintage Skin".

My name is Molly Green and I was in Grade 8 when I was assigned this for a Digital Boss project.

Dandys World was created by Qwelver, who is the creator, developer, and creative director of BlushCrush Studio, the Roblox studio behind Flavour Frenzy and a former Dandys World producer.

Thank you for looking at my Dandys World project!
