💪 Welcome to my Information page! 💪

Dandy's World is a multiplayer survival game created by BlushCrunch Studio. Players complete machines on each floor to help themselves progress to higher floors.

Toons have six mastery quests. When completed, they receive a special skin called a "Vintage Skin", which is grey, black and white.

This website was created by DandysWorldsBiggestFanEverYall, who was in Grade 8 when assigned this for a Digital Boss project.

Dandy's World was created by Qwelver, who is the creator, developer, and creative director of BlushCrunch Studio, the Roblox studio behind Flavour Frenzy and a former Dandy's World producer.
