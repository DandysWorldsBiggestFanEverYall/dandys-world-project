🌸 CHARACTERS 🌸

Welcome to my Characters page!

There are many different types of characters in Dandy's World! Every toon has their own role, abilities and way of helping the team. Explore the categories below to learn more!

✨ TOON CATEGORIES ✨

⭐ Main Toons

These toons have one more ability than all other toons and one less heart in exchange for another ability.

💗 Support Toons

These toons help others with stealth, extraction speed, skillchecks, speed and stamina using their abilities.

⚡ Extracting Toons

These toons have higher extraction speed, higher skillcheck or both.

🏃 Distracting Toons

These toons have higher stamina, speed or both, allowing them to distract Twisteds and protect others.

🎭 Just There

These are the toons who don't have an ability that directly benefits themselves or others in the game.

😚 MY FAVOURITE TOONS 😚

🌙 Astro

Astro is one of my favourites because of his ability to support others with fast stamina regeneration. During blackouts, he also lights up with a beautiful moon display!

🎲 Gigi

Gigi is a favourite because of her ability to gamble for items, as well as her good extraction abilities.

🤗 Goob

Goob is a favourite because he can pull others towards him if they're in danger or need help reaching the elevator.
