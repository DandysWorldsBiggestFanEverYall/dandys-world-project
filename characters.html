😊 Characters 😊

😉 Welcome to my Characters page! 😉

There are many types of characters in Dandys World. There are main toons, support toons, extracting toons, distracting toons and event toons. Every toon fits into a category. Some of the toons who don't fit into a category are the toons that are labeled "just there".

Main toons = These toons have one more ability than all other toons and they have one less heart for exchange of another ability.

Support toons = These toons have the ability to help others gain stealth, extracting speed, skillcheck, speed and stamina with the use of their ability.

Extracting toons = These toons have higher extraction speed or higher skillcheck or both.

Distracting toons = These toons have higher stamina or speed or both so they can protect others from Twisteds.

Just there = The toons who can't benefit themself or others in the game.

😚 Favourite Toons 😚
