💎 TRINKETS 💎

⭐ Welcome to my Trinkets page! ⭐

Trinkets are special items that give toons different advantages during a run. They can improve stats, provide useful items, highlight objects and even help protect you!

✨ WHAT CAN TRINKETS DO? ✨

⚡ Better Stats

Increase extraction speed, skillcheck, stealth, stamina and other useful stats.

🎁 Give Items

Some trinkets provide useful items to help you during your run.

🎟️ Earn Tapes

Certain trinkets can help you obtain tapes to spend in Dandy's Elevator Shop.

🔦 Highlight Items

Some trinkets help you locate items and make exploring easier.

🔧 Help Machines

Certain trinkets can give machines a helpful kick-start.

🛡️ Protection

Some trinkets provide ways to help protect your toon.

📦 WHERE DO TRINKETS COME FROM?

🛒 Dandy's Lobby Shop

Some trinkets can be purchased from Dandy's Lobby Shop.

🧪 Research Capsules

Other trinkets are unlocked by completing a toon's research. This involves encountering their Twisted and collecting research capsules.
